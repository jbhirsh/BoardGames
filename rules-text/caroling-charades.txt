Caroling Charades
Holiday charades with carols and holiday songs
House rules written for The Game Room from the publisher’s own description of the game. This is not the
publisher’s rulebook; where the cards or the box say otherwise, they win.
The idea
Each card names a holiday carol or song. The box describes it as “if Taboo and Name That Tune had a
baby”: easy to play, a bit competitive, for 2 to 8 players, and when you are done with charades you can
play it again as a drawing game.
How to play (charades)
Split into two teams; with two players, take turns performing for each other. On your turn, draw a card
and start the timer (a minute works well). Act out the song title without speaking while your team
guesses. A correct guess wins the card; if time runs out, the card goes back under the deck. Teams
alternate, and the team with the most cards after an agreed number of rounds wins.
Variations
Name That Tune: hum the melody, no words. Taboo: describe the song without saying any word from its
title. Drawing: sketch clues instead of acting, with no letters or numbers. Mix them up by switching each
round.
