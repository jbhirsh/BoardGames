Truth or Dare Tower
The block-stacking tower game with a truth or a dare on every block
House rules written for The Game Room from the publisher’s own description of the game. This is not the
publisher’s rulebook; where the cards or the box say otherwise, they win.
The idea
The classic tower-building game redesigned for date night. Every block is printed with either a truth to
answer or a dare to do. Play as a couple, or with multiple couples if you dare.
Setup
Build the tower from all the blocks in levels of three, laying each level crosswise to the one below it.
How to play
Take turns. On your turn, use one hand to slide a block out from any level below the top complete level.
Read the truth or dare printed on it, then answer honestly or do the dare. Place the block on top of the
tower, finishing a level of three before starting a new one, crosswise to the level below.
Whoever makes the tower fall loses the round; by house rule, the others pick a dare for them. Rebuild
and play again.
