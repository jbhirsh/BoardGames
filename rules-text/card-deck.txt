Card Deck
Twelve games for a standard 52-card deck
House rules written for The Game Room. These are the common rules; plenty of tables play their own variations, so
agree on any before you start.
Using the deck
Every game here uses one standard 52-card deck; leave the jokers out. Unless a game says otherwise,
aces are high, the dealer shuffles and deals one card at a time clockwise starting on their left, and the
deal passes to the left after each hand.
Each game has its own rules sheet: pick it from the tabs on the rules page. The rules assistant has read
all of them, so you can ask it about any game here.
The games
Euchre: 4 players in two partnerships · about 30 minutes.
Spades: 4 players in two partnerships · about 45 minutes.
Hearts: 3 to 5 players, every player for themselves · about 30 minutes.
Crazy Eights: 2 to 7 players · about 15 minutes.
Rummy: 2 to 6 players · about 30 minutes.
President: 4 to 8 players · about 30 minutes.
Spoons: 3 to 8 players · about 10 minutes.
Egyptian Ratscrew: 2 to 6 players · about 15 minutes.
Speed: 2 players · about 5 minutes.
Golf: 2 to 6 players · about 20 minutes.
Go Fish: 2 to 6 players · about 15 minutes.
Klondike Solitaire: 1 player · about 10 minutes.
