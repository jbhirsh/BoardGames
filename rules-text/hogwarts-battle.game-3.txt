Gameplay
Gameplay proceeds with the same four steps (for
full details see pages 6-10 of the Game 1 rule book).
STEP 1. Reveal and resolve Dark Arts events.
STEP 2. Resolve Villain abilities.
Players must defeat all eight Villains. In Game 3
you will have to face two active Villains at a
time. You may choose to assign all your

Remember to use your new Hero ability.
STEP 4. End your turn.
When you defeat a Villain replace it with the
next one from the top of the stack. There should
always be two Villains face up.
