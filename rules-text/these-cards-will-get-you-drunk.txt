These Cards Will Get You Drunk
ASM Games — a drinking game for ages 21+
House rules written for The Game Room from the publisher’s own description of the game. This is not the
publisher’s rulebook; where the cards or the box say otherwise, they win.
The deck
100 cards for any group of two or more; the box suggests 2 to 8, but more can play. The cards compete
you against each other, call votes, and let you screw your friends over.
How to play
Shuffle the deck and put it face down in the middle. Moving clockwise, take turns drawing a card and
reading it out loud. Follow the instructions on the card to determine who has to drink. Some cards set up
a challenge or a vote; some can be kept and played later, exactly as the card says.
There are no winners: keep going until you run through the deck or want a break. The point is to have
fun and enjoy each other’s company.
House note
For ages 21 and up. Any drink works, including water or something without alcohol, and anyone can sit
out a drink. Drink responsibly and never drive after playing.
