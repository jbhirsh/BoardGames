Far-Out Questions
A conversation deck of far-out questions for unusual conversations
House rules written for The Game Room from the publisher’s own description of the game. This is not the
publisher’s rulebook; where the cards or the box say otherwise, they win.
What’s in the box
55 question cards of far-out questions, made to get you laughing together rather than talking about
anything serious. Some from the deck: What cartoon world do you wish you could live in for a week? If
you could uninvent something, what would you erase from history? If you were head of product
development at a fast-food chain, what food abomination would you create? What weird thing would you
make socially acceptable if you could?
How to play
Made for two, and it works with a group of friends too. Shuffle the deck. Take turns drawing a card and
reading the question out loud, then everyone answers. There are no right answers and no points:
follow-up questions and tangents are the point. Skip any card you like.
Other ways to use it
Ad-lib: change a question to suit the people playing, or use one as a cue to invent your own far-out
question. Text a question to your partner during the day, or send just your answer and let them guess the
question. Keep a deck in the car or on the coffee table for a quick pick-me-up.
